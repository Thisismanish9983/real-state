Our Story

Founded on Transparency, Driven by Distinction

Established in 2008, EstatePro was founded to revolutionize how high-value properties are discovered, marketed, and acquired. We recognized that luxury homebuyers and investors demanded more than typical property listings—they needed trusted architectural insights, discreet advisory, and meticulous data analysis.

Over the past decade and a half, we have grown from a boutique Beverly Hills advisory to a premier nationwide presence managing transactions across New York, Miami, Aspen, Austin, Chicago, and Seattle.
