Privacy Policy

Your privacy is paramount to us. Learn how EstatePro gathers, protects, and manages your personal information.

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

EstatePro ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and confidentiality of individuals who visit our website, inquire about listed properties, or utilize our advisory services. This Privacy Policy describes our practices concerning data collection, usage, sharing, and security.

By accessing or using our website, submitting inquiry forms, or interacting with our real estate advisors, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in several ways, including:

  • Directly Provided Information: When you complete a contact form, schedule a property viewing, request valuation services, or subscribe to market reports, we collect details such as your full name, email address, telephone number, and preferred property criteria.
  • Inquiry & Transaction Data: Details regarding properties you save as favorites, properties you inquire about, and specific tour scheduling requests.
  • Device & Usage Data: Technical information including IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, and timestamps gathered automatically to optimize site performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

EstatePro uses personal information solely for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Connecting you directly with licensed advisory partners for requested properties.
  • Scheduling private in-person or virtual walkthroughs.
  • Delivering requested market analyses, off-market opportunities, and updates.
  • Personalizing your browsing experience and remembering your saved favorite properties.
  • Ensuring security, compliance, and fraud prevention across our digital services.

Zero Data Selling: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal contact details to third-party marketing brokers or advertisers.

4. Data Storage & LocalStorage

Our website uses modern browser localStorage capabilities strictly to store your saved favorite properties on your local device. This data remains on your browser and is not transmitted to advertising trackers. You can clear your saved properties at any time by clearing your browser cache.

5. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We only share information under strict parameters:

  • Assigned Real Estate Advisors: With designated listing agents and partners representing the properties you explicitly inquire about.
  • Service Providers: Secure technology partners facilitating website hosting, encrypted communications, and email deliverability under confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by applicable federal, state, or municipal real estate disclosure statutes.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You maintain full control over your personal information. You have the right to request access to the data we hold, ask for corrections, or request complete deletion of your records from our client management database at any time by contacting our Privacy Officer.
